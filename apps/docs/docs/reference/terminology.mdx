## DNS Basics \{#dns-basics}

| Term             | Also known as | Definition                                                                         |
|------------------|---------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **DNS sinkhole** | —             | Answering a blocked name with an address that leads nowhere, so the request stops. |
| **DNS Resolver** | Unbound       | pfSense's Unbound-based DNS service.                                               |

## Lists and Scopes \{#lists-and-scopes}

| Term                    | Also known as                | Definition                                                                                                          |
|-------------------------|------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **block list**          | —                            | A list of names or addresses to block, added by URL or from the search.                                             |
| **popular-list search** | —                            | The WebGUI search of widely used block lists, like AdGuard Home's picker.                                           |
| **saved list**          | —                            | The last good copy of a block list, kept on the firewall.                                                           |
| **scope**               | —                            | Where a block list applies: the whole network, a VLAN, or a single device.                                          |
| **global lists**        | —                            | Block lists that apply to every client on the network.                                                              |
| **SafeSearch**          | Safe search, restricted mode | A search engine's safe mode, forced for every device in a scope by answering the engine's names with its safe host. |
| **rebind exception**    | private-domain               | A domain allowed to answer with a private address despite DNS rebinding protection.                                 |

## Alerts \{#alerts}

| Term           | Also known as | Definition                                                 |
|----------------|---------------|------------------------------------------------------------|
| **alert rule** | —             | A list and scope whose blocks send a pfSense notification. |

## Bypass Protection \{#bypass-protection}

| Term                   | Also known as | Definition                                                                                                                                                                 |
|------------------------|---------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **bypass protection**  | Bypass        | Optional, interface-scoped controls that keep clients on DNS paths Sinkhole can inspect by redirecting plain DNS or blocking common encrypted-DNS and tunnel alternatives. |
| **plain-DNS redirect** | —             | The optional Bypass setting that sends devices' ordinary TCP and UDP port 53 queries to the firewall.                                                                      |
